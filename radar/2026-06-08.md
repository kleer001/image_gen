Note: the 4-day window is thin. This digest also surfaces two models that fell between earlier sweep windows — one released June 3 (not picked up by the focused multi-view June 4 digest) and two released in early–mid May (before the first radar's ~14-day lookback).

## Image base models

- **Ideogram 4** (9.3B params, non-commercial gated license) — Ideogram's first open-weight T2I model. Released June 3, 2026; within the June 4 window but not covered (that digest was narrowly focused on multi-view). Single-stream 34-layer DiT; Qwen3-VL-8B as text encoder (13-layer hidden-state features). Fills a real gap in this stack: **best-in-class multilingual text rendering** + explicit bounding-box layout and color-palette controls — Flux and SDXL both struggle at in-image text. Weights are gated on HF; fp8 transformer ~9 GB + fp8 Qwen3-VL encoder ~8 GB; with ComfyUI sequential offloading both stages fit comfortably in 24 GB. The nf4 variant (~4.5 GB transformer) offers more headroom at a quality tradeoff. ComfyUI: native support merged (Comfy-Org/ComfyUI PR #14259 + official workflow template); official custom nodes at `ideogram-oss/ComfyUI-Ideogram4`; `Comfy-Org/Ideogram-4` repo provides pre-converted fp8-scaled weights. Fits 24 GB: **yes** (fp8: comfortable with offloading; nf4: easy). Sources: https://huggingface.co/ideogram-ai/ideogram-4-fp8 · https://ideogram.ai/blog/ideogram-4.0 · https://github.com/ideogram-oss/ideogram4. Verdict: **try** — if design/typography work is on the agenda; non-commercial license only.

- **HiDream-O1-Image** (8B params, MIT license) — Released May 8, 2026; fell just before the first radar's ~14-day lookback and was never reported. Reasoning-driven T2I: model performs chain-of-thought before denoising, producing better compositional adherence on complex prompts. Dev-2604 variant debuted at #8 in the Artificial Analysis T2I Arena at launch. fp8 full pipeline ~10 GB VRAM. ComfyUI: native support merged (PR #13817); custom node `Saganaki22/HiDream_O1-ComfyUI` adds LoRA support and patch-grid smoothing. Layout and skeleton conditioning available in IP pipeline. Different capability angle from Flux (reasoning-guided vs. guidance-distilled); MIT license is unusually permissive for an 8B foundation model. Fits 24 GB: **yes** (fp8 ~10 GB). Sources: https://huggingface.co/HiDream-ai/HiDream-O1-Image · https://github.com/HiDream-ai/HiDream-O1-Image · https://github.com/Saganaki22/HiDream_O1-ComfyUI. Verdict: **try** — MIT license, low VRAM cost, distinct reasoning capability.

## Video models

- **SANA-WM** (2.6B params, open weights, NVlabs) — Released May 16, 2026; fell just before the first radar's window. Minute-scale 720p I2V with **6-DoF camera control** (camera path specified as trajectory, tilt, pan, zoom). Hybrid-linear-attention architecture (frame-wise Gated DeltaNet + softmax). Weights on HF: `Efficient-Large-Model/SANA-WM_bidirectional`. At 2.6B the model itself fits trivially in 24 GB (< 6 GB). Caveat: the *distilled* fast variant targets NVFP4 on RTX 5090; base inference on RTX 3090 should work but speed/quality tradeoffs are unconfirmed from primary sources. No ComfyUI integration found at sweep time. License not confirmed from model card — check before use. Use case is narrow: camera-path-guided long-form video, not general prompt-to-video. Fits 24 GB: **yes** (model weights only; full pipeline VRAM unconfirmed on 3090). Sources: https://huggingface.co/Efficient-Large-Model/SANA-WM_bidirectional · https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.15178 · https://github.com/NVlabs/Sana. Verdict: **watch** — unique 6-DoF capability but no ComfyUI node and 3090 performance unconfirmed.

## ComfyUI nodes / tools

- **ComfyUI-Ideogram4** (official) — `ideogram-oss/ComfyUI-Ideogram4` provides structured JSON caption input, bounding-box layout, color-palette controls, and the asymmetric CFG dual-model guidance flow Ideogram 4 requires. Required companion to the Ideogram 4 weights. Source: https://github.com/ideogram-oss/ComfyUI-Ideogram4.

- **HiDream_O1-ComfyUI** — `Saganaki22/HiDream_O1-ComfyUI` adds LoRA loading between model loader and sampler plus patch-grid smoothing node. Installable via ComfyUI Manager ("HiDream O1" search). Source: https://github.com/Saganaki22/HiDream_O1-ComfyUI.

## Explicitly excluded / debunked

- **WAN 2.7**: Multiple tech blogs claim an April 2026 release with first-and-last-frame + video editing, but no weights exist under huggingface.co/Wan-AI (only 2.1 and 2.2 repos confirmed). Appears to be cloud-API only or pre-release speculation. *Unverified* — excluded.
- **FLUX.2 [dev]** (32B): Released November 2025. Does not fit in 24 GB unquantized (~32 GB fp8); would need GGUF INT4 (~16 GB) with severe quality loss for a 32B model. Outside the window and outside the hardware ceiling. FLUX.2-Klein-9B (9B, Nov 2025) does fit but is also months outside any window.
- **Cosmos 3 Nano** (16B, OpenMDW-1.1): Released May 31, 2026. Targets "workstation-grade compute like RTX PRO 6000" per NVIDIA docs; dual-tower (8B reasoner + 8B generator co-loaded) pushes the 24 GB limit. License is research/non-commercial. Primary use case is physical AI / robotics, not creative image generation. Skip.

## Bottom line

Two models fell through sweep windows and are worth acting on now: **Ideogram 4** (9.3B, non-commercial, fp8 fits 24 GB — fills the text-in-image/layout gap Flux and SDXL both lack, ComfyUI native) and **HiDream-O1-Image** (8B, MIT license, ~10 GB fp8 — reasoning-driven generation, unusually permissive license, ComfyUI native). Both are low-friction adds to the stack. The 4-day window itself is quiet; nothing new dropped after June 4. **SANA-WM** is watch-only: 6-DoF camera control is genuinely novel but lacks ComfyUI integration and has unconfirmed 3090 performance.
