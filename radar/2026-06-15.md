## Video models

- **Wan 2.7** (14B, Apache-2.0, late March 2026) — Status upgrade from **"excluded/unverified"** (June 8 digest) to **watch**. Multiple secondary sources now confirm open weights on ModelScope (Alibaba's hub) and GitHub; HuggingFace weights not present, consistent with June 8. Capabilities: 1080p / up to 15 s, text-to-video + image-to-video + video editing, first-and-last-frame conditioning, subject referencing, native audio output. The same WAN hardware red flag (CLAUDE.md) applies: 14B class, confirm VRAM profile before running (WAN 2.2 I2V 14B uses ~18–20 GB on this rig). Primary fan/secondary sources only this sweep — primary ModelScope model card not directly fetched. Fits 24 GB: **yes (with quantization / block swap)** — same envelope as installed WAN 2.2 I2V 14B. Sources: https://wan.video/ · https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/wan-2-7-coming-soon-major-upgrade/. Verdict: **watch** — verify ModelScope card exists and VRAM profile before pulling; do NOT run without the usual WAN pre-flight check.

- **MOVA** (32B total / 18B active MoE, Apache-2.0, January 29 2026) — First mention in any sweep (released January 2026; missed by all prior radar runs). OpenMOSS + MOSI Intelligence; asymmetric dual-tower (video + audio) fused via bidirectional cross-attention; generates synchronized video **and** audio in a single inference pass, covering lip-sync, environmental sounds, and music. Two model sizes: MOVA-360p and MOVA-720p. VRAM: 24 GB recommended for 720p; 12 GB minimum via layerwise offloading (≈42 s/step on RTX 4090 — expect slower on 3090). Directly comparable use-case to the installed Ovi model (video + generated audio), but 32B vs. Ovi's 5B base; quality ceiling is plausibly higher. **No ComfyUI integration found** at sweep time — inference via PyTorch scripts only. Sources: https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOVA-720p · https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOVA · https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.08794. Fits 24 GB: **yes (720p) / yes (360p with offload)**. Verdict: **watch** — fills the Ovi quality-ceiling gap once a ComfyUI node appears; script-only pipeline is workable but not as frictionless.

## ComfyUI nodes / tools

- **Nvidia_RTX_Nodes_ComfyUI** (official NVIDIA) — Adds **RTX Video Super Resolution** as a native ComfyUI node, plus NVFP4 and FP8 precision modes across sampling. Announced at GDC 2026; primary source is NVIDIA's own blog. RTX VSR claims 30× faster 4K video upscaling vs. "competing local upscalers" (*unverified benchmark — NVIDIA blog*). Hardware note: NVFP4 (2.5× faster, 60% less VRAM) **requires RTX 50 Series**; the RTX 3090 (Ampere) gets the **FP8 path** (1.7× faster, 40% less VRAM), which is still a meaningful win. Distinct from the community `comfyui-deno-custom-nodes` (flagged June 9 as "watch"), which also wraps RTX VSR — this is the official NVIDIA implementation. Directly fills the validated-upscale slot in `scaffolds/video_post_upscale_interp.json`. Sources: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-ai-garage-flux-ltx-video-comfyui-gdc/ · https://www.runcomfy.com/comfyui-nodes/Nvidia_RTX_Nodes_ComfyUI/rtx-video-super-resolution. Verdict: **try** — FP8 path works on 3090; install replaces the ESRGAN upscale step in the video post scaffold.
