# Model Radar — 2026-06-03
Window: since repo start (no prior digest; ~last 14 days). GPU ceiling: 24 GB (RTX 3090; no `nvidia-smi` present, written ceiling used).

## Image base models
- **Qwen-Image-2.0** (7B, license TBD — predecessor was Apache-2.0) — beats FLUX.1 on DPG-Bench (88.32 vs 83.84) at ~half the params, native 2K, unified gen+edit. **Open weights NOT yet released** as of this window. Fits 24 GB: yes (when released; 7B easily quantizes). Source: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image. Verdict: **watch** (no weights to pull yet).
- Nothing else net-new with open weights this window. FLUX.1-dev (installed) remains the workhorse open foundation.

## Video models
- **Wan2.1-VACE-14B** (14B, Apache-2.0) — all-in-one **reference-to-video (R2V)** + video-to-video + masked-V2V editing: Reference-Anything / Swap-Anything / Animate-Anything. This is the open analog to Seedance's multi-image reference conditioning and the single biggest gap-filler for character-consistent shots. Note: official VACE is on the **2.1** base — there is **no official Wan2.2-VACE** yet. Fits 24 GB: **quantized only** (fp8/GGUF, same envelope as the installed WAN 2.2 I2V). Source: https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.1-VACE-14B. Verdict: **try**.
- **LTX-2.3** (DiT audio+video foundation, open license, fp8 + NVFP4 + distilled variants on HF) — synchronized **video + audio** in one model, t2v and i2v, up to ~20s clips, near-real-time with the distilled model. Great fast-iteration / rough-cut engine to complement slow WAN renders. Fits 24 GB: **quantized only** — full bf16 ~26 GB; fp8 ~13 GB; vendor guide pushes 32 GB for "production quality" fp8-cast+distilled, while a community guide claims 8 GB with heavy optimization (*both unverified, vendor/SEO blogs*). Practical read: runs on 24 GB via fp8/distilled with a quality/length tradeoff. Sources: https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.3-fp8 , https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-Video. Verdict: **try**.
- **Wan2.2-S2V-14B** (14B, speech/audio-to-video, MoE) — drives lip-sync/performance from an audio track; directly relevant to the dialogue-scene use case ("that's a lot of jacket… and where's yours?"). Fits 24 GB: **quantized only** (fp8/GGUF). Source: https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-S2V-14B. Verdict: **watch → try** if dialogue shots are wanted.

## LoRAs (for installed families)
- **Photorealistic Skin — No plastic [FLUX]** — Flux.1-dev, targets the exact "plastic/pasted-on face" failure the source video is about. Fills a real gap (the stack has many *style* LoRAs but no realism/skin detailer). Source: https://civitai.com/models/1157318/photorealistic-skin-no-plastic-flux. Verdict: **try**.
- **Portrait Engine FLUX1.D — Detailed Skin v2.0** — Flux.1-dev; natural pores/texture, dramatic cinematic lighting + film grain out of the box. Pairs with the gray-bg + soft-light convention. Source: https://civitai.com/models/2067704/portrait-engine-flux1-d-detailed-skin-lora. Verdict: **try**.

## ComfyUI nodes / tools
- **ComfyUI-nunchaku** (SVDQuant 4-bit INT4/FP4 inference for Flux/Qwen) — large VRAM/speed wins on the 24 GB ceiling; v1.2.0 adds INT4 for 20-series. Source: https://github.com/nunchaku-ai/ComfyUI-nunchaku.
- **city96/ComfyUI-GGUF** — native GGUF quantization for DiT/Flux; standard path to squeeze 14B video + Flux under 24 GB. Source: https://github.com/city96/ComfyUI-GGUF.
- **Wan2GP** (deepbeepmeep) — "GPU-poor" multi-model runner: Wan 2.1/2.2, Qwen-Image, HunyuanVideo, LTX-Video, Flux under one optimized harness. Useful reference for low-VRAM presets. Source: https://github.com/deepbeepmeep/Wan2GP.

## Bottom line
Two things are worth acting on: **Wan2.1-VACE-14B** (reference-to-video — the open answer to Seedance-style multi-image character locking, and the highest-leverage video add) and a **Flux skin-realism LoRA** ("No plastic" / Portrait Engine) to kill the plastic-face problem at the image stage. **LTX-2.3** is a strong fast-iteration/audio-video complement (runs on 24 GB quantized, with tradeoffs), and **Wan2.2-S2V** is the path to lip-synced dialogue if/when wanted. No new open *image base* model is pullable this window — Qwen-Image-2.0 is watch-only until weights drop.
