# Model Radar — 2026-06-29
Window: since 2026-06-22 (7 days). GPU ceiling: 24 GB (RTX 3090; remote session — written ceiling used).

## Image base models

- **Krea 2 Raw / Turbo** (12B DiT, "Krea 2 Community License", released 2026-06-23) — Krea's open-weights image model in two variants: Raw (unaligned mid-training checkpoint, ideal for LoRA fine-tuning without stylistic bias) and Turbo (8-step distilled, post-trained, production-ready). Turbo at BF16 ≈16 GB VRAM — fits 24 GB directly. Raw at BF16 ≈24.76 GB — borderline on 24 GB; community fp8 quants (`Winnougan/Krea-2-Base-Turbo-NVFP4-FP8-INT8`, `AlperKTS/Krea2_FP8`) reduce to ~12 GB. License permits free commercial use for teams with <$1M revenue and <50 seats; larger commercial use requires a separate agreement — not Apache 2.0. Comparable parameter count to Flux.1-dev (both 12B) but different architecture and reportedly broader aesthetic range. Native ComfyUI support landed in v0.26.0 (also June 23) as partner nodes; loads via standard diffusion model nodes. Source: <https://huggingface.co/krea/Krea-2-Turbo> / <https://huggingface.co/krea/Krea-2-Raw> · ComfyUI blog: <https://blog.comfy.org/p/krea-2-open-source-models-are-now>. Fits 24 GB: **yes** (Turbo BF16) / **quantized only** (Raw, fp8 community quant). Verdict: **try** — Turbo is the practical install; Raw is the fine-tune base for future Krea2 LoRAs.

## Video models

Nothing net-new released in this 7-day window. However, a standing TODO is resolved (see below).

- **LTX-2.3 VRAM clarification** (not a new release; 22B, released 2026-03-05, standing TODO from prior digests) — Community consensus this window settles the conflicting guide claims in TODO.md: `fp8_scaled` requires sm_89+ (RTX 40xx Hopper architecture) and is **incompatible with the RTX 3090** (sm_86 Ampere). Confirmed viable paths for this rig: **int8 quantized** (fits 24 GB, supports up to 1080p) or **mxfp8_block32** (RTX 30xx-compatible alternative). BF16 distilled variants also work for short clips. LTX-2.3 is uniquely relevant because it generates synchronized audio+video in a single pass — a capability gap the installed WAN and HunyuanVideo pipelines do not fill natively. Source: <https://ltxworkflow.com/models> · RTX 3090 fp8 limits: <https://docs.bswen.com/blog/2026-03-15-rtx-3090-quantization-limits/>. Fits 24 GB: **quantized only** (int8 or mxfp8_block32; fp8_scaled is a hard no on sm_86). Verdict: **try** (with int8 path) — resolves the open TODO; worth trialing for audio+video in one pass.
