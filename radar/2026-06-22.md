## Video models
- **Bernini-R 1.3B** (ByteDance, ~2.6 GB, Apache 2.0, released 2026-06-09) — lightweight video editor fine-tuned from Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B. Supports style transfer, watermark/subtitle removal, local region editing, and reference-to-video (R2V) in a single model pass. Tiny model (~2.6 GB) — trivially fits 24 GB, nothing close to the WAN hardware red-flag. Native ComfyUI support landed in **v0.25.0 (2026-06-16)** via `ComfyUI-BerniniR`; requires `ComfyUI-GGUF` for text-encoder loading. Reuses installed WAN UMT5-XXL encoder (confirm exact node wiring before running). Source: https://github.com/bytedance/Bernini · ComfyUI node: https://github.com/neuregex/ComfyUI-BerniniR. Fits 24 GB: **yes** (1.3B). Verdict: **try** — fills the lightweight video-edit gap with minimal VRAM cost; a good counterpart to the heavy WAN 2.2 I2V pipeline.

- **SCAIL-2** (Z.ai / Zhipu AI, Apache 2.0, released 2026-06-09) — end-to-end character animation and motion transfer from a driving video to a reference character image; no skeleton extraction or intermediate pose representation required. Handles single-character animation, cross-identity replacement, multi-character scenes, and zero-shot animal/non-humanoid generalization in one model. Backbone is **WAN 2.1 14B**, so VRAM at bf16 peaks ~47 GB (hard no at 24 GB); a GGUF community quantization exists (`realrebelai/SCAIL-2_GGUF`) but a validated fp8 path for ComfyUI has not been confirmed in primary sources. Native ComfyUI support added in **v0.25.0 (2026-06-16)**. Source: https://huggingface.co/zai-org/SCAIL-2 · Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10804. Fits 24 GB: **quantized only** (GGUF, not yet validated for this rig). Verdict: **watch** — directly relevant to the open gap for identity-consistent character animation (currently covered only by the unvalidated DWPose scaffold); monitor for a confirmed 24 GB fp8/GGUF path.

## ComfyUI nodes / tools
- **ComfyUI v0.25.0 + v0.25.1** (2026-06-16) — key additions: native **Bernini-R** and **SCAIL-2** support (day-0 on both); **Depth Anything 3** integration (SOTA monocular depth estimation, directly upgrades depth-ControlNet input maps for both the installed Flux depth CN and SDXL depth CN); **10-bit video I/O**; `PreviewGaussianSplat` / `PreviewPointCloud` nodes for 3D previews. v0.25.1 adds Kling V3-Turbo (closed API, out of scope). Source: https://docs.comfy.org/changelog.

- **Civitai ComfyUI Nodes** (2026-06-16, ~160 nodes) — in-graph Civitai model/LoRA/ControlNet browser with one-click loaders, plus a generation-history sidebar that pulls past outputs back onto the canvas. Reduces the round-trip of manually looking up CivitAI model IDs and download URLs. Available via `comfy-registry` and GitHub (`civitai/civitai-comfy-nodes`). Source: https://civitai.com/articles/31483. Verdict: **try** — low-risk quality-of-life node for active LoRA discovery sessions.

- **ComfyUI-DepthAnythingV3** — community wrapper for Depth Anything 3 (ICLR 2026); enables better monocular depth maps for the stack's installed depth ControlNets (Flux depth CN + SDXL depth CN). Drop-in swap for the DA2 preprocessor. Source: https://github.com/PozzettiAndrea/ComfyUI-DepthAnythingV3. Verdict: **try** — single node install, direct quality uplift for depth-conditioned workflows.

## Bottom line
Two things are worth acting on this window. **Bernini-R 1.3B** (try): a 2.6 GB Apache-2.0 video editor from ByteDance that runs trivially in 24 GB and fills the lightweight V2V/R2V gap — install `ComfyUI-BerniniR`, confirm UMT5-XXL encoder wiring, and test style transfer and local edits. **ComfyUI v0.25.0** (try): update the comfyui/ checkout to get native Bernini-R/SCAIL-2 support and the Depth Anything 3 depth preprocessor, which gives a free quality bump to every workflow that uses the installed depth ControlNets. **SCAIL-2** is the most interesting character-animation development in this window but its 14B WAN backbone needs a validated GGUF/fp8 path before it runs on this rig — watch until confirmed.
